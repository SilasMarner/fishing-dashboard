Upload handwritten log pages — {{ locations[selected] }}

Snap a photo or scan of each handwritten page (JPEG/PNG/WebP). The page is read, every catch mapped to a known species, and proposed log entries are generated. Nothing is saved until you review and click Import. Tide / weather / solunar conditions are backfilled automatically from NOAA for each entry's date.

OCR.space (default) is free and reads handwriting well — best for most log pages and tag forms. Tesseract is free and on-device but weaker on cursive. Claude vision is the most accurate for messy handwriting but uses Anthropic credits. OCR text is structured into entries by the free Groq model.

For very large archives, use the command-line bulk importer instead — see scripts/import_logs.py --help.
